We do not collect any personal data from people who use TtaraON Mobile (the "app"). The app has no account or login, and we run no server that receives videos or personal data. The app does not request the INTERNET permission and has no feature that sends video or audio off the phone.

1. Personal data we collect

None. We do not receive or store your videos, audio, name, contact details, location or device identifiers. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Permissions and why the app uses them

PermissionWhyWhen
CameraTo record the class and crop the picture to follow the teacherOn the ready screen and while recording
MicrophoneTo record sound with the videoWhile recording
NotificationsTo show recording and saving statusWhile recording or saving
Receive boot completedTo finish saving a recording that was interrupted when the phone turned offWhen the phone starts
Foreground serviceTo keep recording and saving from being stopped midwayWhile recording or saving
Billing (Google Play)To check your subscription status through Google PlayOn the subscription screen and when recording starts

The camera and microphone are used only while recording. Without them, recording is not available.

3. Where your videos are stored

  • Recorded videos are saved only on your phone or on the SD card you choose.
  • Video, audio and teacher detection results are never sent off the phone. The app has no upload feature, and teacher detection runs only on the device.
  • Videos stay in your storage until you delete them.
  • If you move a video elsewhere yourself, for example with the gallery or a share feature, that transfer is your own action and is not covered by this policy.

4. Payments (subscription)

  • Recording requires a subscription. New subscribers get a 14-day free trial, after which the monthly plan renews automatically. The price for your country is shown on the subscription screen and in the Google Play payment sheet before you subscribe.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play. The app does not connect to the internet itself; it only checks through the Google Play app whether you are subscribed. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • You can cancel at any time, including before the trial ends, from the app's subscription screen or in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Personal data related to payments is handled under Google's privacy policy.

5. Students and other people in the video

TtaraON is a recording tool for teachers. Class videos may contain the faces and voices of students or other people. The user who records is responsible for obtaining consent from students, parents and the school or academy, and for following applicable laws when recording, storing or sharing videos. We do not receive any videos.

6. Children

The app is a tool for teachers and is not directed at children.

7. Retention, deletion and third parties

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. We do not provide personal data to third parties or outsource its processing. Payments are handled directly by Google Play (section 4).

8. Your rights and contact

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. For any questions, please contact us.
