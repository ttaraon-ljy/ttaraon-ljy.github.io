TtaraON Mobile (the "app") does not collect any personal data from the people who use it. The app has no account or login, and we run no server that receives videos or personal data. The app does not request the INTERNET permission and has no feature that sends video or audio off the phone.

1. Personal data we collect

The app itself never sends us any information. We do not receive or store your videos, audio, name, contact details, location or device identifiers. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

However, if you choose to email us — for example with "Report a problem (email)" in Settings — we receive your email address and your message. "Report a problem" opens your email app with a draft that already contains your phone's manufacturer and model, Android version, app version, how far you got in the in-app guide, and a short summary of your recent recordings (numbers such as recording length, sound levels and detection counts, and a log of the saving process). You can read and delete any of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. No video or audio is attached. We use this information only to reply and to fix problems, and delete it within six months after the issue is resolved.

2. Permissions and why the app uses them

PermissionWhyWhen

The camera and microphone are used only on the ready screen and while recording. Without them, recording is not available.

3. Where your videos are stored

  • Recorded videos are saved only on your phone or on the SD card you choose.
  • Video, audio and teacher detection results are never sent off the phone. The app has no upload feature, and teacher detection runs only on the device.
  • Videos stay in your storage until you delete them.
  • If you move a video elsewhere yourself, for example with the gallery or a share feature, that transfer is your own action and is not covered by this policy.

4. Payments (subscription)

  • Without a subscription, each recording can be up to 20 minutes for free. A monthly subscription removes the time limit and renews automatically. The price for your country is shown on the subscription screen and in the Google Play payment sheet before you subscribe.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play. The app does not connect to the internet itself; it only checks through the Google Play app whether you are subscribed. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • You can cancel at any time from the app's subscription screen or in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. After cancelling, you can keep using it until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Personal data related to payments is handled under Google's privacy policy.

5. Students and other people in the video

TtaraON is a recording tool for teachers. Class videos may contain the faces and voices of students or other people. The user who records is responsible for obtaining consent from students, parents and the school or academy, and for following applicable laws when recording, storing or sharing videos. We do not receive any videos.

6. Children

The app is a tool for teachers and is not directed at children.

7. Retention, deletion and third parties

Apart from emails you send us yourself (section 1), we hold no personal data. We delete those emails within six months after the issue is resolved. We do not provide personal data to third parties or outsource its processing. Payments are handled directly by Google Play (section 4).

8. Your rights and contact

Apart from emails you have sent us, we hold no personal data about you. To see or delete an email you sent, or for any other question, please contact us.
