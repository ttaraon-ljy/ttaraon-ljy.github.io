Lecture recording that follows the teacher — TtaraON

Ttaraon is a one-person software business from Korea. We make TtaraON, which follows the teacher in front of the board and crops the video automatically, so a single fixed camera is enough to record a class.

Apps

TtaraON Mobile (Android)

Put a spare phone on a tripod and start recording. The app follows the teacher, crops the picture, and saves the cropped video right away. Video and audio never leave the phone, and teacher detection runs on the device. New subscribers can try it free for 14 days.

Coming to Google Play. The app is currently in Korean.
